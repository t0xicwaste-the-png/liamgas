About Us

Welcome to LiamGas, the premier destination for real-time web communication.

Founded in 2026, LiamGas was created to address the need for a lightweight, fast, and community-driven chat platform. Our tech stack is built on Python, Flask, and PostgreSQL, ensuring a robust environment capable of handling high concurrency and seamless user experiences.

Our Vision

We believe in the power of direct communication. By removing bloatware and complex interfaces, LiamGas provides a distraction-free zone for students, developers, and creatives to share ideas instantly.

Technology

We deploy on Render using a serverless database architecture. Our front-end utilizes asynchronous JavaScript and XML (AJAX) to fetch new messages without requiring page reloads, simulating a web-socket-like experience with exceptional reliability.
