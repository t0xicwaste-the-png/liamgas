March 12, 2026

Connection Pooling and Postgres Stability

Today we rolled out a major infrastructure update to handle database connection scaling. Previously, when the platform experienced periods of low traffic, our Neon Serverless Postgres instance would aggressively sleep, causing "SSL connection unexpectedly closed" errors for the next incoming user.

We've implemented aggressive connection recycling (280 second TTL) within our SQLAlchemy engine options to ensure that stale connections are silently refreshed before they can trigger a 500 Server Error. This change drastically improves uptime without increasing our baseline compute footprint.

March 7, 2026

v1.0 Platform Launch

LiamGas v1.0 is officially live! We've successfully migrated from a local SQLite database to a fully managed PostgreSQL cluster hosted on Render. The new asynchronous chat fetching protocol handles concurrent users smoothly, providing a real-time experience without the overhead of WebSockets.
